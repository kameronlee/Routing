# Venue Notes

## ACM SIGSPATIAL

Emphasize spatial algorithms, raster/vector fusion, OD-scale benchmarking,
detour-constrained search, and reproducibility. Strong evidence: clear algorithm
description, scalability/runtime analysis, baselines, and released code/data
configuration.

## BuildSys

Frame around built-environment sensing/modeling and operational decision
support. Strong evidence: building/canopy/shadow data pipeline, uncertainty
analysis, and discussion of deployment constraints.

## IEEE ITSC

Emphasize pedestrian routing, transportation network constraints, detour
budgets, and route choice metrics. Strong evidence: larger OD benchmark,
runtime, failure rates, and comparison to transportation baselines.

## IJGIS

Emphasize GIScience methods, raster alignment, spatial sampling, uncertainty,
and reproducible geographic analysis. Strong evidence: rigorous spatial data
handling, sensitivity tests, and transparent limitations.

## Computers, Environment and Urban Systems

Frame as urban analytics and computational planning support. Strong evidence:
policy relevance, environmental justice discussion, remote audit cases, and
clear limitations around modeled exposure.

## Urban Climate

Emphasize urban microclimate interpretation. Strong evidence: multiple weather
conditions, validation or careful plausibility checks, and strong treatment of
UTCI/shadow model uncertainty.

## Building and Environment

This venue likely expects stronger environmental/comfort validation. Add field
transects, multiple conditions, and comparison against measured microclimate
before targeting a full paper.

## Environment and Planning B

Emphasize planning methodology, spatial decision support, equity, and
computational urban design implications. Strong evidence: broader case study
coverage and planning-relevant interpretation.
